## 14. FAQ block (4–6)

1. **Can I sell a game I made with Claude Code?** Anthropic's terms assign you its rights, if any, in Claude Code's output on Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise and API. Free doesn't include Claude Code. Not legal advice.
2. **Does my Claude plan change anything?** Yes, in two ways. The Commercial Terms (Team, Enterprise, API) add an IP indemnity for paid use and bar Anthropic from training on your content. The Consumer Terms (Pro, Max) don't.
3. **Who owns code written by Claude Code?** As between you and Anthropic, Anthropic hands you whatever rights it has. Whether copyright exists in AI-written code is a separate legal question; link the copyright guide.
4. **Do I have to disclose Claude Code on Steam?** Valve says efficiency gains from AI dev tools are "not the focus" of its AI disclosure, which targets AI content players consume. itch.io's disclosure field includes Code.
5. **Does Anthropic train on my game's code?** On Pro and Max, yes, unless you opt out in settings. Under the Commercial Terms, Anthropic "may not train models on Customer Content".
