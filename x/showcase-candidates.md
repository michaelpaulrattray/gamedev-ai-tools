# Showcase candidates: AI-made indie games (researched 2026-10-21)

Bar: at least the visual level of Crowned In Steel and Bring It, a real playable loop, AI actually used, dev not anti-gen-AI. Excluded: @brehm_shaun, @builtbysketch, @WhistioHQ. I'm listing only 2 that clear the bar plus 1 conditional pick. Quality beats quantity. Nobody was contacted.
Screenshots are in /workspace/x/media/showcase/ (box, outside this repo).

## 1. SOULLOOP by @SoulloopGame (Echo Decks Studio). STRONGEST
- Game: steampunk roguelite FPS with deckbuilding. Free Steam demo, in Next Fest Oct 19-26. https://store.steampowered.com/app/4959240/SOULLOOP/
- Visuals: torch-lit brass and stone corridors, a consistent steampunk look, and an ornate card UI. This is the closest match to Crowned In Steel. It works as a 5-second clip.
- AI use (Steam AI disclosure on the store page): "textures, card art, capsule and key art, some 3D meshes, sound effects and voice-over" made with AI assistance. Code and design are hand-built. Post: "this was an AI picture. now it's a room you can walk through in my game. 6 simple steps, 57 tries" https://x.com/SoulloopGame/status/2112187664321347773 (video). Named tools: none stated publicly that I could find.
- Real creator: the X account is new (created 2026-10-08), but it posts near-daily devlogs (boss fixes, the gun system, robots "cut into 17 parts", for example https://x.com/SoulloopGame/status/2112604627258769484). The Steam page is under Echo Decks Studio. Godot dev.
- Openness: strong. It builds in public, asks followers questions, and replies to other devs. A partial breakdown exists (the AI picture to room post). No full tool breakdown yet. Engagement is still tiny (0 to 7 likes per post).
- Verdict: passes the bar. It's playable now and looks good. Ask them which image and 3D tools they used.
- Screens: /workspace/x/media/showcase/soulloop-steam-1.jpg, soulloop-steam-2.jpg, soulloop-x-thumb.jpg

## 2. Nightshoal by @RUSuper (itch: ProFactor), 1920s fishing and exploration, Godot. PASSES (creator settled in pass 2)
- Game: free alpha for Windows and Linux on itch, https://profactor.itch.io/nightshoal (author "ProFactor", itch AI disclosure: Code, Graphics, Sounds, Text). A Steam demo is "planned".
- Visuals: a stylized archipelago, lighthouse and ocean, with a moody night menu and a cohesive look. Well above Paper Route and roughly at the bar.
- AI tools: Claude Code (Opus 5.5) for code, Tripo for 3D animals and characters, ElevenLabs for sound and music, Blender MCP for model fixes, Astra for textures.
  - https://x.com/RUSuper/status/2107788400732545103 (Code: Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code; 3D animals: Tripo; Sound & music: ElevenLabs)
  - https://x.com/hakimieiqbal/status/2104910311983616340 (week 9 breakdown: multi-agent Claude sessions, ElevenLabs, Astra, TripoAI, Blender MCP; 309 likes, 29,878 views)
- WHO MADE IT: two accounts both say "I'm building" it. @RUSuper has 43 followers, bio "Developing games with AI", and a weekly Reddit devlog. VibeCodingList and Stefan_3D_AI credit @RUSuper. @hakimieiqbal (2,297 followers, an AI-business account that posts heavy reply-guy volume) posts the weekly "Week N" threads and links the same itch page. It could be one person with two accounts, or it could be reposting. Confirm before featuring. Credit RUSuper/ProFactor by default.
- Openness: strong. Weekly public devlogs, and an itch page that asks for feedback. An existing breakdown is the week 9 thread above.
- Screens: /workspace/x/media/showcase/nightshoal-itch-1.jpg, nightshoal-itch-2.jpg, nightshoal-x-thumb.jpg

## 3. (Conditional) "Ghost of Tsushima" browser recreation by @ashen_one
- Post: https://x.com/ashen_one/status/2093450103079981455 (video, 523 likes, 47,507 views). Also an Attack-on-Titan-style Unity mini game: https://x.com/ashen_one/status/2097808059254951972 (449 likes, 62,245 views, "Play the entire game below for free").
- AI tools (from the post itself): Higgsfield CLI (GPT Image 2), Meshy API multi-image-to-3D, Blender CLI animation, Kimi K3 coding, Minimax H3 video for the loading screen, Suno music. The Unity game used the Meshy API, Blender MCP and Unity CLI. That counts as its own breakdown.
- Visuals: the best of everything I found (golden-hour field, samurai and horse). But both games recreate existing IP (Ghost of Tsushima, Attack on Titan). They're short tech demos, and the account is an AI content creator (55.6k followers), not a game studio. I didn't verify the play link.
- Verdict: great for a 5-second clip, but there's IP risk and it isn't an original game. Only use it if Mike is fine with that.
