### What this means for our first posts
1. **Make the first post a joke about being a first post, in character, under about 12 words.** CIA and Bluesky did it with their own signature phrase. Ours (since 21 Oct) is comparing things.
2. **Then say plainly what we are.** The posts that held up (Bluesky's welcome, Raycast's launch, early Cursor) say what the thing is and what happens next, with no hype words.
3. **Lead with something free and useful.** For tool accounts, the best early posts were things people could actually use (Tripo in the GPT Store, open-sourced code, Scenario's plugin), not "we're live 🚀".
4. **Everything that failed shares the same tics:** 🚀, "thrilled to announce", "INSANE", contests, hashtags, links in the body, and posts that don't say what the account is. All of those are already banned in `docs/x-voice.md`.
5. **Caveat:** most of these accounts were famous before their first post, or became famous later for reasons beyond the post. A new account with no followers should expect small numbers on day one either way. The aim is that anyone who lands on the profile from a reply gets it in five seconds.
